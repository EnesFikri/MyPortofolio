Dengue Hotspot

  • Dengue Hotspot is a population health initiative aimed at identifying and predicting high-risk dengue transmission areas using epidemiological data and environmental indicators. The project leverages data analysis and geospatial mapping to support early warning systems and targeted public health interventions. It is designed to assist healthcare authorities in resource allocation, outbreak prevention, and community awareness strategies. I contributed to study design, data interpretation, and integration of clinical and public health perspectives to ensure relevance and applicability in real-world settings.

DiaLens

  • Dialens is a digital health solution designed to enhance early detection and monitoring of chronic diseases through data-driven insights and user-friendly interfaces. The project integrates patient-reported data, clinical parameters, and AI-assisted analysis to support personalized care and clinical decision-making. With a focus on usability and scalability, Dialens aims to bridge gaps between patients and healthcare providers, improving disease monitoring, adherence, and long-term outcomes. My role involved contributing to clinical concept development, data structuring, and ensuring medical accuracy in the platform’s design.
